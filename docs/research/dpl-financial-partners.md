# DPL Financial Partners: The Fee-Only Route for Annuities and Insurance

*Research date: September 26, 2026. DPL does not publish its full pricing, so confirm every number in writing.*

**Why this matters:** Daniel Vazquez (Altruist) named DPL as the "fee-only" alternative to keeping a friendly B/D. You would convert your insurance and annuity business to advisory fees instead of commissions, and compliance gets lighter.

---

## 1. What DPL is

- A platform (called **Avenew**) of **commission-free annuities and insurance** from **20+ carriers**, built for fee-only and hybrid RIAs (confirmed).
- Products include MYGAs (fixed annuities), variable annuities, RILAs, fixed indexed annuities, income annuities (SPIA/DIA), and life insurance (confirmed).
- Named carriers include Allianz, American Life, Ameritas, Aspida, Corebridge, Equitable, Guaranty Income Life, and Integrity Life (confirmed).
- **Scale:** more than **$5B** in annuities on the platform (October 2025), and 8,500+ RIA firms with access directly or through Orion or Black Diamond (confirmed).
- **Backing:** a **$23M Series C (December 2024)** led by Eos Ventures, with TIAA Ventures participating (confirmed).
- **Owns a broker-dealer:** in September 2023 it bought AnnuityFix and **Johnstone Brokerage Services (JBS)**, a FINRA member. JBS offers DPL's securities products (confirmed).

## 2. How you'd get paid

- **No commissions.** You bill a client-agreed **advisory fee on the contract value**, the same way you bill on other managed assets (confirmed).
- **You don't need an insurance license.** DPL is the **agent of record** on the policies (confirmed).
- Billing and data feeds officially **integrate with Orion, Black Diamond, Envestnet/Tamarac, and SS&C**. **Altruist is not listed.** Ask how you'd bill annuity assets if Altruist is your custodian and billing platform.

**About Daniel's "roughly 10% haircut":** that's his estimate; DPL doesn't publish anything like it. The real trade-off is timing:

| | Commissioned annuity | DPL fee-based annuity |
|---|---|---|
| Up-front pay | Large, paid once | None |
| Ongoing pay | Small trail | Your advisory fee on the contract value, every year the client stays |

So year-one revenue drops, and later-year revenue can be higher than a trail. Model this with your own book before deciding.

## 3. What DPL costs you

- **Membership fee: starts at $1,000 a year, per firm, not per seat.** It scales with firm AUM, and 3- and 5-year memberships are discounted (confirmed by a DPL spokesperson, 2024). Your tier at about $40M is **not public**.
- Membership covers the platform, product tools, client materials, and support. Optional consulting is priced separately (confirmed).
- Client-side costs: commission-free products usually carry lower internal costs, but annuities can still have **fees, expenses, and surrender charges** (DPL disclosure).

## 4. Your existing annuities (the key question for a breakaway)

DPL runs a **Breakaway Accelerator** program built around the AnnuityFix and JBS acquisition (confirmed):
1. **Annuity Review** analyzes your clients' existing contracts. Just under $1B of contracts had been uploaded since its late-2025 launch. For each contract it flags whether an exchange to a commission-free product would help the client.
2. **If exchanging is better**, a 1035 exchange moves the client into a DPL product. Fees start once the new policy is funded.
3. **If keeping the old contract is better** (good riders or guarantees, surrender period), DPL "helps transition those policies from the advisor's existing broker-dealer," apparently to its own B/D, JBS.

**Why this could matter to you:** if JBS can hold your legacy variable annuities and trails, you might not need a friendly B/D at all. The fee-only RIA plus DPL route could replace the hybrid B/D route. DPL **does not publish** how trails work after the move to JBS: who keeps them, what share, and whether you must keep your Series 6/7. Ask these first.

## 5. Questions to ask DPL (888-327-0049)

1. What's the membership fee for a firm with about $40M in AUM, on 1-, 3-, and 5-year terms?
2. If I custody at **Altruist**, how do I bill advisory fees on annuity contract value? Is there a data feed?
3. Under the **Breakaway Accelerator**, can my current VAs move to **Johnstone Brokerage**? What happens to the **trails**: do I keep them, at what payout, and do I need to keep my Series 6/7 (or enroll in FINRA's MQP)?
4. For life insurance, where no advisory fee applies, how am I paid? A planning fee? Does DPL keep the commission?
5. Does DPL or JBS receive any compensation from the carriers? (Relevant to your ADV conflict disclosures.)
6. Timeline and cost for Annuity Review on my current book.
7. Which carriers and products are available in **New York**? NY often has a narrower product shelf.

## 6. How this fits the decision Daniel described

| | Full hybrid (friendly B/D + Altruist) | Fee-only (Altruist + DPL) |
|---|---|---|
| Commissionable products | Sold through the B/D (PKS, PCS, M.S. Howells, Mutual) | Converted to fee-based through DPL |
| Your pay on annuities | 100% of the commission payout from the B/D grid | Advisory fee on contract value |
| Compliance | Heavier: dual registration, B/D supervision | Lighter: RIA only |
| Licenses | Keep Series 6/7 plus an insurance license | Neither needed for DPL products; MQP can preserve the 6/7 |
| Fixed costs | B/D fees, E&O, FINRA and state registrations | DPL membership (from $1k a year) |
| Rule of thumb (Daniel) | Better if commissions are 25–30%+ of revenue | Better if commissions are occasional |

*General information, not tax, legal, or compliance advice. Have your compliance firm review the fee-on-annuity structure and your ADV disclosures.*
