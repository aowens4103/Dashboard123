# RIA Custodians for a New Small RIA: Minimums, Platform Fees, and Who to Call

*Research date: September 23, 2026. Most custodians do not publish their RIA pricing; several say outright that it is "relationship-based." Treat every number below as a starting point and get the custodial agreement and full fee schedule in writing before you sign.*

---

## 1. Short answer

**For a new RIA without $50M+ in assets, your realistic shortlist is Schwab, Altruist, Interactive Brokers, Axos, and LPL (fee-only custody).** All five publicly state no AUM minimum. Schwab, Altruist, and IBKR also charge no custody or platform fee. Fidelity, BNY Pershing, and Raymond James generally want $50M to $100M+ before they onboard a new RIA, and they price privately.

"No custody fee" does not mean free. Custodians that charge nothing make their money elsewhere, so compare **total cost of ownership**:

| Where the custodian earns | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Cash sweep spread** | The yield your clients get on the default sweep, compared with a money market. This is the largest hidden cost at most zero-fee custodians. |
| **Ticket charges** | Commissions on mutual funds outside the no-transaction-fee list, bonds, broker-assisted trades, and block trades. |
| **Fund and ETF revenue sharing** | Payments from fund sponsors to be on the custodian's shelf. **Fidelity has charged a 5% transaction fee (capped at $100) since June 1, 2026 on purchases of ETFs from sponsors that don't pay it asset-based fees** (confirmed). |
| **Account fees** | IRA annual fees, account closing, ACAT transfer-out, wires, paper statements, and custody of alternatives. |
| **Platform / admin fee** | A basis-point or flat fee on the RIA's assets. It is usually charged only below a size threshold or in exchange for bundled technology. |

## 2. Two kinds of custodian

| | "Open-door" custodians (no minimum) | "Relationship" custodians |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Schwab, Altruist, Interactive Brokers, Axos, LPL (fee-only custody), TradePMR, Betterment Advisor Solutions | Fidelity, BNY Pershing, Raymond James, SEI, Goldman Sachs Custody Solutions |
| AUM minimum | None published | Commonly $50M–$100M+. Raymond James's new joiners average about $600M. |
| Platform fee | Usually none. Betterment charges 25 bps; LPL and TradePMR negotiate. | Private. Often a fee under a threshold, or bps pricing (SEI starts at 0.10%). |
| Service model | Mostly digital or service-team based. Schwab and Altruist offer transition help. | Dedicated relationship manager, more complex assets, more custom pricing |
| Best fit | Solo or small RIA, breakaway with under $50M, fee-only | $100M+ firms, alternatives-heavy books, or firms that want a single-relationship white-glove model |

## 3. Firm-by-firm profiles

Legend: **Confirmed** = in a public source (linked in Sources). **Not public** = you have to ask.

### Open-door custodians (best fit for a new solo RIA)

**Charles Schwab Advisor Services**
- AUM minimum: **None.** Schwab's pledge is "no AUM minimums and no custody fees" for independent RIAs (confirmed). CEO Rick Wurster repeated in August 2026 that Schwab won't charge RIAs custody fees.
- New 2026 fee: a **$5-per-account fee on certain block trades**, and on block orders that need special handling from the Block Desk, **effective June 1, 2026** after RIA pushback delayed it from March 2 (confirmed). It targets large RIAs placing big block trades. A small RIA using model or automated trading is mostly unaffected, but ask how your rebalancing tool routes orders.
- Scale: $5T+ in RIA assets. It has the deepest ecosystem of technology integrations.
- Watch: Schwab makes its money on cash sweep spreads and trading, so ask for current sweep yields. The **Schwab Advisor Network** referral program (a source of client referrals) requires $500M+ in RIA AUM and is raising the client referral floor to $5M. It isn't relevant for a new firm.
- Full per-account fee schedule: in the "Pricing Guide for Advisor Services." Ask for the current PDF, since the public page wouldn't load for us.

**Altruist** (being acquired by Vanguard)
- AUM minimum: **None.** Custody fee: **none** (confirmed).
- Included free: performance reporting, fee billing, rebalancing, client portal, and portfolio accounting. That replaces software that can cost $5k–$15k+ a year elsewhere.
- Optional **Altruist One** plan, launched July 28, 2025: **1 bp per month per household (0.12% a year)**. It raises the brokerage sweep yield from 0.20% to 2.00% APY and adds model marketplace discounts, automated tax management, and no transaction fees on mutual funds or fixed income (confirmed). You can pass the fee to clients or absorb it, and you choose which households get it.
- **Vanguard deal:** announced **August 26, 2026, for $4.6B in cash**, expected to close later in 2026. Vanguard says Altruist will stay a standalone business with the same operating model, brand, and leadership (confirmed). Kitces notes it ends Altruist's "no asset-manager affiliation" independence, but it may keep ETF access free of the pay-to-play fees other custodians are adding.
- Past fee episode: in April 2024 Altruist announced IRA fees, then walked them back. The IRA maintenance and Roth conversion fees are now $0, and the retirement account closure fee is $75 (confirmed).
- Scale: 6,000+ advisors (May 2026). It was the fastest-growing custodian in the 2025 T3 study.

**Interactive Brokers (IBKR) – advisor custody**
- AUM minimum: **None.** No custody fees, ticket charges, or technology, platform, or reporting fees (confirmed; stated again by IBKR in September 2026).
- Clients pay IBKR's published commissions and earn "up to 3.13%" on USD cash (confirmed, September 2026).
- Scale: $100B+ in RIA assets and about 3,600 advisors.
- Best fit: cost-sensitive, ETF, options, or international books. The service model is more self-directed than Schwab's or Altruist's.

**Axos Advisor Services**
- AUM minimum: **None published** (confirmed via third-party guides).
- Known fees: **$45 per broker-assisted trade**; **$62.50 per alternative-asset position per quarter** (confirmed). Other fees are in its "Standard Fee Schedule." We couldn't extract the PDF, so ask for it.
- Scale: about $37B under custody or administration. Bank parent (Axos Bank), so banking and lending for advisors are in-house.

**LPL Financial – fee-only RIA custody**
- AUM minimum: **None** (confirmed).
- Pricing is customized and comes in two models: an all-inclusive **platform fee in the "low- to mid-single-digit basis points,"** or à la carte (confirmed, 2021 launch article; current rates not public).
- Fee-only RIAs pay **nothing extra to be multi-custodial**. Hybrid RIAs that use LPL's B/D for brokerage but custody elsewhere pay a bps fee (confirmed).
- Why it matters for you: if you end up using LPL's B/D for trails and commissions, one platform can hold both the brokerage and the RIA accounts. Also see the B/D doc: admin fees drop at $75M and are waived at $250M on LPL's corporate RIA.
- Ask for the "RIA Miscellaneous Account and Service Fees Schedule" (lpl.com/disclosures/fee-schedules). The PDF blocked automated access.

**TradePMR (owned by Robinhood since February 2025)**
- AUM minimum: **no stated minimum.** It "evaluates each interested advisory firm individually" (confirmed).
- Pricing, all negotiated and **not public** (confirmed): (1) per ticket, (2) basis points on assets, or (3) a flat platform fee.
- Transition money: a **Growth Incentive Program (June 2026)** with incentives on net new assets and forgivable loans. Robinhood also pays clients for the "pain of repapering" (confirmed).
- Includes the Fusion platform, with AI tools at no extra cost.

**Betterment Advisor Solutions**
- AUM minimum: **None.** **Platform fee: 25 bps** (third-party source; Betterment doesn't publish current advisor pricing). Your advisory fee sits on top (confirmed).
- It's a bundled robo platform with custody, trading, and reporting, not a traditional custodian. It's simple, but 25 bps is expensive relative to Altruist or Schwab once you're past about $10M.

### Relationship custodians (usually $50M–$100M+)

**Fidelity Institutional (Clearing & Custody Solutions)**
- AUM minimum: **typically $50M–$100M** for a new RIA, unless you're joining a firm already on Fidelity (third-party guides; not published by Fidelity).
- Pricing: "**relationship-based**." Fidelity has **no public starting price list** and declined interviews on it (Financial Planning, May 2026, confirmed).
- Last publicly reported small-firm fee: **$2,500 a quarter ($10,000 a year) for firms under $15M in assets** (reported 2020; **not confirmed as current**).
- New in 2026: the 5% ETF transaction fee (capped at $100) on non-paying sponsors' ETFs, from June 1, 2026 (confirmed).
- Firms under $30M have historically faced limits on alternatives and fund transfers.

**BNY Pershing (Pershing Advisor Solutions)**
- AUM minimum: **about $100M** (down from $250M) (confirmed via third-party guides).
- Pricing options: variable, **monthly subscription**, or zero-transaction-fee (confirmed). Rates are **not public**.
- Best fit: $100M+ firms with complex or alternative assets. Many friendly B/Ds also clear through Pershing, so ask whether your B/D and RIA accounts could share a platform.

**Raymond James (RIA & Custody Services)**
- AUM minimum: **not published**, but new joiners average about **$600M+**. Its separate RJFS independent-RIA platform raised its minimum from $30M to $50M (confirmed).
- It can custody your RIA and clear your B/D business on one platform.
- You already emailed **Alan Brum** (business development consultant covering NY) on 9/23 about the B/D side. Ask him about RCS custody pricing in the same thread.

**SEI Private Trust Company**
- Custody fee **starts at 0.10%** and "eliminates nearly every transaction fee." Only five items are billed separately: wires, overdrafts, stop payments, account closing, and special assets (confirmed, July 2026). Rates fall below 0.10% as the relationship grows.
- AUM minimum: **not public.** It has 654 advisory firms and $138B.

**Goldman Sachs Custody Solutions (formerly Folio Investments)**
- Won InvestmentNews Custodian of the Year (June 2026). It targets "growth-focused" and established RIAs.
- AUM minimum: **not published, but its contact form's lowest AUM choice is "$100–250 Million"** (checked 9/23/2026), which suggests about a $100M floor. Fees: **not public.**

## 5. Questions to send every custodian

1. **AUM minimum** for a new RIA, and any **small-firm or under-threshold fee** (amount, and the level at which it goes away).
2. **Platform, admin, or custody fee**: bps or flat, what it covers, and breakpoints as assets grow.
3. **Ticket charges**: ETFs, stocks, mutual funds (on and off the no-fee list), fixed income, block trades and special handling, broker-assisted trades.
4. **Account fees**: IRA annual fee, account closing, ACAT transfer-out, wires, paper statements, alternative-asset custody.
5. **Cash**: today's default sweep yield, money market alternatives, and how you earn on client cash.
6. **Fund and ETF shelf**: any fees tied to sponsors that don't pay revenue sharing (e.g., Fidelity's 5% ETF fee), and the no-transaction-fee list.
7. **Included technology**: billing, performance reporting, rebalancing, client portal, CRM and planning integrations. Which are free and which are paid?
8. **Hybrid support**: can I custody my RIA with you while keeping a separate B/D for trails and commissions? Any fee for that, and can the B/D accounts sit on the same platform?
9. **Transition support**: repapering help, ACAT fee reimbursement, transition credits or forgivable loans, and whether they're tied to asset targets.
10. **Exit terms**: termination fees, notice period, and data export if I leave.

## 6. Suggested path (general info, not legal or investment advice)

1. **Get written quotes from 3 no-minimum custodians:** Schwab, Altruist, and one of IBKR or Axos. Use §5's questions, and ask each for a total-cost estimate on your actual book (number of households, assets, typical holdings, cash levels).
2. **Compare on cash.** For a small fee-only book, the sweep-yield difference is often larger than every other fee combined. Compare Altruist One (0.12% fee, 2.00% sweep) against Schwab's and IBKR's default cash terms.
3. **If you stay hybrid,** check whether your friendly B/D's clearing firm (often Pershing) or LPL can hold both sides. One platform means one set of statements and less repapering. Ask the B/Ds you contacted on 9/23 which RIA custodians they work with.
4. **Before you sign with Altruist,** read the Vanguard closing terms, expected later in 2026, and any changes to the custodial agreement.
5. **Revisit Fidelity or Pershing** once you're past about $50M–$100M, if you need alternatives, complex assets, or a dedicated relationship team.
